Seven-Day Awakening Retreat in Vietnam

Set aside seven days to practice fully.

Morning practice, afternoon rest, and evening Satsang.
Over seven days, let bodily experience and inner understanding slowly come together.

When daily life is filled with one thing after another, practice can easily become only a small part of the schedule. This time, we set aside seven full days and give that time back to practice.

About This Retreat

Morning

Practice asana, pranayama, and meditation with Nandaji

Afternoon

Time for rest, hot springs, nature, and cultural experiences

Evening

Satsang for discussing yoga texts, the day’s practice, and questions that have not yet become clear

Satsang is the shared study time in the evening. Nandaji draws from the Yoga Sutras and connects them with asana, pranayama, and daily life, guiding questions and discussion. The body gathers experience during the day; conversation helps organize understanding at night, and the next morning we return to practice with new observations.

The natural environment, hot springs, and spacious rest time at Alba Wellness Valley provide a quiet and steady setting for daily learning. Over seven days, we are not rushing to accumulate more, but allowing each practice, rest period, and discussion to settle.

What You May Take Home

  • A continuous and complete experience of yoga practice
  • Clearer observation of body, breath, and mind
  • Ways to connect yoga texts with daily practice
  • A rhythm of practice that can continue back in daily life

Teacher

Nandaji

Nandaji has devoted many years to yoga practice and teaching, including asana, pranayama, meditation, and yoga philosophy. He is able to translate teachings that may seem subtle or difficult into learning that students can experience directly.

Inquiry before answers: In class, he does not rush to give answers. Through repeated inquiry, he guides students to find understanding through their own body, breath, and practice.

A long-term relationship of practice: Many students continue studying with him not only to learn more asanas, but to gradually develop the ability to observe and understand themselves through repeated practice.

Bringing philosophy into daily life: Evening Satsang is an important part of learning. Nandaji brings together yoga texts, the day’s practice, and questions from participants, so that yoga becomes not only class content, but a practice that continues into daily life.

Departure

Hoi An Extension (optional, Dec 24–26, 2026)

After the seven-day retreat, you may choose to continue to Hoi An for two nights at a five-star hotel, slowing down and experiencing another side of Central Vietnam.

The extension is priced separately. Room choice and arrangements can be confirmed when registering. Some activities are optional and may be selected separately.

Twin room: NT$5,500/person Single room: NT$7,500/person
Day 1 | Hue - Lang Co Beach - Hoi An Ancient Town

Travel south from Hue to Hoi An, stopping at Lang Co Beach to take in the Central Vietnam coastline.

In the afternoon, a guide will lead a walk through Hoi An Ancient Town, introducing its streets, temples, and riverside culture. After the guided walk, you may explore freely, stroll along the river, or visit cafes and local shops.

The evening is free. You may enjoy the lantern-lit town, visit the night market, and browse local crafts and small shops.

Day 2 | My Son Sanctuary

In the early morning, visit My Son Sanctuary, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and an important religious center of the ancient Champa Kingdom. Champa culture was strongly influenced by Hinduism, especially the worship of Shiva.

Among ruins surrounded by forest, learn about the history, beliefs, and architecture of the Champa Kingdom. Admission to My Son Sanctuary is included.

After returning to Hoi An, you may rest or choose from the following optional activities at your own expense:

  • Cam Thanh coconut basket boat experience: ride a traditional basket boat through water coconut groves
  • Hoi An Memories outdoor performance
Day 3 | Return

After breakfast, check out and transfer to Da Nang International Airport according to the group flight schedule.

Extension Includes / Does Not Include

Does not include: Meals during the extension and personal expenses, Cam Thanh coconut basket boat experience, Hoi An Memories outdoor performance, and other optional activities not listed.

FAQ

How much yoga experience is needed?

At least three months of regular yoga practice is recommended.

What will we study each day?

Morning classes include asana, pranayama, and meditation. Some evenings include Satsang, where Nandaji connects yoga texts, the day’s class, and participants’ questions for further discussion.

What is Satsang?

Satsang is an evening time for shared study and dialogue. Nandaji draws from the Yoga Sutras and the day’s practice, shares yogic perspectives, and responds to questions about asana, pranayama, and daily practice. No prior background in yoga philosophy is required.

Will there be sightseeing?

The main retreat centers on yoga practice, Satsang, and rest. It includes a cultural tour in Hue, nature walks, and outdoor activities. There are no shopping stops and no packed sightseeing schedule.

What is special about the accommodation?

Alba Wellness Valley is set in a natural environment. During the stay, participants may use the hot springs and will receive one 50-minute massage.

Is international airfare included?

International airfare is not included. Participants are responsible for booking their own flights to Vietnam and arriving at the designated airport according to the group transfer schedule.

Can I join only the seven-day main retreat?

Yes. The two-night Hoi An extension is optional. Participants who do not join may go to the airport after check-out on Dec 24 or arrange their own onward travel.

Can I register alone?

Yes. Room options include a 25 sqm single room, 35 sqm single room, and 70 sqm single wooden villa. You may also choose a twin room and ask the organizer to help arrange a roommate.

Can I choose my room type?

Yes. You may choose a room type according to your needs. Availability is limited and rooms are reserved in the order deposits are completed.

Are meals vegetarian?

The main retreat includes daily vegetarian brunch. Group dinners are mainly vegetarian. If you have allergies or special dietary needs, please note them when registering. Final arrangements depend on the hotel’s availability.

Can I join if I have an old injury or special condition?

Please describe your current physical condition, old injuries, surgery history, or medical restrictions when registering. The organizer will help assess suitability based on the course format. If you have acute pain, recent surgery, or a major medical condition, please obtain medical clearance first.
